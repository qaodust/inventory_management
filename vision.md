This app is being developed internally for two sole proprietors who are starting a bulk import business and are looking to track their costs, inventory, and manufacturers.

Requirements:
-Web hosted and mobile friendly. Hosting provider is railway with database on railway as well. Will be accessed from PC, android and IOS by the two people involved.
-Account authentication.
-Manufacturer tracking, including the ability for the two owners to: rate the quality of items provided, track delivery times and shipping fees, rate ease of use with the manufacturer.
-Product tracking: including amount of each product available, the cost per unit (shipping costs to be included in the total cost per unit), and the profit per unit once each unit is sold.
-Individual sales. Each product will have a "goal" price to sale, but will be made individually. So every sale needs to include the route of sale (facebook, ebay, in person, etc) and the amount sold for.
-Simple profit/loss metrics, as well as product metrics to include total sale time from the time a shipment arrives until the last unit is sold, total profit/loss, and track routes of sale.
